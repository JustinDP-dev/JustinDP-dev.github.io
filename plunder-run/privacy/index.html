Plunder Run Privacy Policy

Effective October 12, 2026

This policy explains what information the Plunder Run mobile game ("the game") uses and why. Plunder Run is made by Loot Table Games ("we", "us"). If you have any questions, email contact@loottablegames.com.

The short version

  • We don't ask for your name, email or any account. There's nothing to sign up for.
  • Your scores and game progress are saved only on your device. We never receive them.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Google collects some device information to show and measure those ads, as described below.
  • We don't sell your information.

Information saved on your device

The game saves the following on your phone or tablet so it works between sessions. It is never sent to us or to anyone else.

WhatWhy
Top-10 leaderboard: scores, the round reached, the date, and the captain names you type inTo show your best runs on the title screen
Whether ads are turned off (for an ad-free purchase)So an ad-free purchase keeps working
Your ad privacy choicesSaved by Google's consent tool so you aren't asked again every time

You can delete all of this by uninstalling the game or clearing its storage in your device's settings.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of the game shows full-screen ads between runs using Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To serve ads, measure them, limit how often you see the same ad, and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the game may automatically collect:

  • Device identifiers, such as your device's advertising ID and app set ID
  • Your IP address, which can be used to estimate your general location (such as your country or city, not your exact location)
  • Interactions with ads, such as when an ad is shown or tapped
  • Diagnostic information, such as crash reports and ad performance data
  • Basic device information, such as the device model, operating system version and language

This information is collected and processed by Google, not by us, and is sent over an encrypted connection. Google's use of it is covered by the Google Privacy Policy. To learn more, see how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Ad-free editions of the game never start the ads SDK, so none of this information is collected there.

Your choices

  • Consent: if you're in a region where consent is required (such as the European Economic Area, the UK or some US states), the game asks for your choices before any ads are loaded. You can change them at any time with the Privacy settings button on the title screen.
  • Personalized ads: on Android, go to Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads) to delete your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads.
  • Playing offline: the game works without an internet connection. While you're offline, no ads are loaded.

Children

Plunder Run is meant for players aged 13 and older and isn't directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we'll help remove it.

Data retention and deletion

Information saved on your device stays there until you uninstall the game or clear its data. Information collected by Google for advertising is kept according to Google's own policies. You can ask Google to delete it through your Google Account and device settings.

Security

The game doesn't send your scores or names anywhere, and ad traffic to Google is encrypted. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we keep the information the game handles to the minimum it needs to work.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the game handles information, we'll update this page and the effective date above. Major changes will also be mentioned in the game's release notes.
